Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 May 2026.

PawAlert (“we”, “the app”) is operated by RabbitQr. This policy explains what personal information PawAlert collects, why we collect it, and how it's stored and shared. We collect only what's needed to run the rescue service and never sell, rent, or trade your data.

What we collect

CategoryExamplesWhy we collect it
Account identifiers Phone number (sign-in via OTP) or Apple ID, optional display name and email Authenticate you, attribute reports/rescues to you, contact you about your rescue
Profile Display name, optional avatar, city, role (reporter / volunteer / both) Show other users who's helping with a rescue
Location Precise GPS at the moment you post a report; coarse location of volunteers when in-app to discover nearby reports Match reports to volunteers in the area
Photos Photos you take or pick when posting a report or marking a rescue complete Document the animal and the rescue outcome
Audio Optional voice note attached to a report Convey condition details that are hard to type
Device tokens APNs / Firebase Cloud Messaging registration tokens Send rescue alerts and status updates
Diagnostics Anonymized crash reports and basic usage events Fix bugs and improve reliability

We do not collect contacts, browsing history, advertising identifiers, health data, financial data, or sensitive demographic information.

How we use it

  • Rescue coordination. Reports are visible to other signed-in users in the area so volunteers can respond. The reporter's display name and the accepting volunteer's display name appear on the rescue card.
  • Notifications. Push notifications go to the reporter when a volunteer accepts/completes a rescue, and to nearby volunteers when a new report is posted within their preferences.
  • Operational hygiene. Reports auto-close after 7 days if no volunteer accepts. Accepted rescues auto-release after ~45 minutes of inactivity. Closed reports older than 90 days are permanently deleted, including attached photos.

Where data lives

PawAlert uses Firebase (Google Cloud) for authentication, database, storage, push delivery, and serverless backend. Data is hosted in Google Cloud's asia-south1 region.

Sharing

  • Other users in your area see your reports (photo, location, note, display name) — that's the rescue model.
  • Cloud Functions read and write your data to coordinate rescues and send pushes.
  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not share data with marketers.
  • Legal compliance. We will disclose data if required by law or to protect users from harm.

Your choices

  • Edit profile. Update your display name, avatar, city, and about text any time from the Profile tab.
  • Notification preferences. Configure radius, severities, and quiet hours from Profile → Notifications.
  • Delete your account. Profile → Delete Account permanently removes your account, releases any in-flight rescue, marks your reports as unresolved, and deletes your FCM tokens.
  • Photos. You can choose camera-only access or grant photo library access — both work with the report flow.
  • Location. Required while-in-use to discover nearby reports. The app does not collect location in the background.

Children

PawAlert is rated 4+ but is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we materially change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and bump the “Last updated” date above. Material changes that affect existing data will be communicated in-app.
