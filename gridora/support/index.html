Common questions

How do I play? Settings → How to Play explains the three rules, notes and hints in under a minute.

How do hints work? A hint first explains where to look, then can show the cell, and only fills it in if you choose Reveal. One hint covers all three steps.

Will I lose my progress? Your progress is stored on this device. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it.

I bought Gridora Pro. How do I get it back? Open Settings → Gridora Pro and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I change the app's language? Settings → Appearance & Language. Gridora is available in ten languages.
