GRIDORA

Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

The short version

Gridora has no account and asks for no personal details. Your games, statistics, awards and settings are stored only on your device. To find crashes and see which features are used, the app sends anonymous usage events and crash reports to Google Firebase. It does not use an advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Who is responsible

Gridora is made by Syed Razack Imran Thajudeen. Questions about this policy or your data: isyedrazackimran@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Every game you play, your statistics, streaks, awards and settings are kept in the app's own storage on your device. They are not uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

What is sent, and why

Usage events (Firebase Analytics). When the app opens, when a game starts or ends (its board size, difficulty, mode, time, mistakes and hints used), when a hint is used, when an award is unlocked, and when a Gridora Pro purchase is started, completed or restored (with where the offer was shown, such as Settings or the hint button). Firebase also records an app instance identifier, your device model, iOS and app versions, and your approximate country, which it derives from your network address. This shows which features are used and where the app needs work.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes, a report with technical details of the crash, your device model, iOS and app versions, and the board size, difficulty and mode being played. This is how crashes get found and fixed.

None of this includes your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. It is not sold and not used for advertising.

Gridora Pro purchases

Gridora Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account; Gridora never sees your name, card or billing details. The app receives only Apple's confirmation of whether Pro is owned on your Apple Account, which it checks on the device to unlock unlimited hints.

Who receives it

Google, which runs Firebase, processes this data on Gridora's behalf. See Firebase privacy and security and Google's privacy policy. Google keeps it for as long as its Firebase retention settings allow, and then deletes it.

Your choices

You can ask for the usage data linked to your app instance to be deleted by emailing the address above. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device and stops all data from being sent.

Children

Gridora is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

Optional rewarded ads are planned. This policy will be updated before they are released, and the effective date above will change whenever it is.
