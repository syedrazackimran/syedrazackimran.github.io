Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. CineFlicx has no sign-up and no login. Your watchlist, ratings, and history live on your device from the moment you open the app.

Where is my watchlist stored? Will it sync to my other devices?

On the device itself, in the app's local database. Because there's no account, your library does not sync between an iPhone and an iPad — each keeps its own. This is a deliberate privacy trade-off: nothing about what you watch is uploaded anywhere.

Will I lose my library if I delete the app?

Yes. Deleting the app removes its local database along with it, and since nothing is stored on a server there's no way to restore it. An iCloud or iTunes device backup taken before deletion will bring the library back when you restore the app from that backup.

Why is my watch time zero, or lower than I expect?

Watch time is calculated from the runtime of the titles you've marked watched. Runtime is filled in when a title's detail screen has been opened, so items saved and marked watched without ever opening them may not contribute yet. Open the title once and the figure will catch up.

Why does Surprise Me suggest something I've already seen?

It shouldn't — anything marked watched is strongly demoted. If it happens, the title probably isn't marked watched in your library. Note that a title being on your watchlist is not the same as being marked watched.

How do recommendations decide what to show me?

From your own ratings and watch history, computed on your device. Genres you rate above your personal average get weighted up; titles you've seen get weighted down. With a new or empty library there's nothing to learn from yet, so early suggestions lean on general popularity and get more personal as you rate things.

Why does the app ask about tracking?

CineFlicx shows ads, and that prompt is Apple's App Tracking Transparency request asking whether ads may use your device's advertising identifier. Choosing Ask App Not to Track is fully supported — you'll still see ads, they just won't be personalised. Nothing about your watchlist or ratings is used for advertising either way.

Why does it want my location?

Only for weather-based suggestions, and only while you're using the app. Decline it and everything else works normally.

A poster or title looks wrong, or something's missing

Film and TV data — titles, posters, cast, release dates, ratings — comes from TMDB, a community-maintained database. Streaming availability comes from JustWatch via TMDB and varies by country. If a detail is wrong at the source, correcting it on themoviedb.org flows through to the app. Email us if something looks wrong in the app but right on TMDB.

Streaming availability doesn't match my country

Availability is region-specific. Set your region in the app so provider listings match where you actually are, rather than defaulting to your device locale.

Release reminders aren't arriving

Check that notifications are enabled for CineFlicx in iOS Settings → Notifications. Reminders fire on a title's announced release date, and those dates do shift — if TMDB updates a date, the reminder moves with it.
