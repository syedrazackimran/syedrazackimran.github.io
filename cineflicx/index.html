CineFlicx

Track what you watch. Discover what's next.

CineFlicx is a movie and TV companion that keeps your watchlist, your ratings, and your viewing history on your own device. No account, no sign-up, no cloud profile — open the app and start using it.

What it does

  • Watchlist and diary. Save films and series, mark them watched, and rate them out of five stars.
  • Your statistics. Total watch time, favourite genres, most active day of the week, and how your taste has shifted.
  • Mood discovery. Pick a mood and get titles that fit it, not a generic popularity list.
  • AI search. Describe what you're after in plain language and let on-device search work out what you mean.
  • Surprise Me. One tap for something you haven't seen, weighted toward the genres you actually rate highly.
  • Release countdowns. See how long until an upcoming film or episode lands, in days and months rather than a bare date.
  • Home screen widgets. Trending and upcoming titles, plus a shortcut straight into AI search.

Recommendations that learn from you

Mood discovery, Surprise Me, and AI search all read your own ratings and watch history to build a taste profile on your device. Genres you rate above your personal average get weighted up; titles you've already seen get pushed down. Nothing about your taste leaves your phone — the profile is computed locally, every time, from data that never gets uploaded.

Privacy

Your watchlist, ratings, and viewing history are stored only on your device. There is no CineFlicx account and no server holding your library. See the Privacy Policy for what the app does and doesn't collect.

Get the app

CineFlicx is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, iOS 18 or later.
