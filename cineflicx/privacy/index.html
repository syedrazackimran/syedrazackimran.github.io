Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026.

CineFlicx (“we”, “the app”) is operated by RabbitQr. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's stored and shared.

There is no CineFlicx account and no CineFlicx server. Your watchlist, your star ratings, your watched history, and your search history are stored on your device and are never uploaded to us. We could not look up what you watch even if asked to.

What stays on your device only

  • Your library — films and series you save, what you've marked watched, and the dates you marked them.
  • Your ratings — the star ratings you give titles.
  • Your taste profile — the genre preferences the app derives from your ratings to personalise Mood, Surprise Me, and AI search. Computed on the device, every time, and never transmitted.
  • Your search history — recent searches, so you can tap one again.
  • Your settings — region, adult-content preference, and onboarding state.

None of the above leaves your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

These are not linked to your identity — there's no account to link them to. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, health data, financial data, or precise location for analytics.

About your searches

Search text is deliberately never sent. Before anything is reported, the query is put through a SHA-256 hash and only the first 12 characters of that hash are kept, alongside the character count. That is enough to see that a search was repeated; it cannot be reversed into what you typed.

Advertising and tracking

CineFlicx shows ads through Google AdMob. When you first open the app, iOS asks whether CineFlicx may track you across apps and websites:

  • If you allow it, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalise ads. Under Apple's definition this counts as tracking, and the app declares it as such.
  • If you decline — the default — the identifier is not used and ads are not personalised. Everything else in the app works identically.

You can change your mind at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Your watchlist, ratings, and viewing history are never used for advertising, whichever you choose.

Location

If you grant location access, the app uses your approximate location while you are using it to fetch local weather for weather-based suggestions. Location is sent to the weather provider to get the forecast; it is not stored by us, not used for ads, and not collected in the background. Declining costs you only that one feature.

Photos

If you save a poster or still to your photo library, the app asks for add-only permission at that moment. It does not read your existing photos.

Notifications

If you enable release reminders, a device push token is registered so the reminder can be delivered. It identifies the device, not you.

Who the data goes to

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config) — crash, performance, and interaction data, and push delivery.
  • Google AdMob — serves the ads, and receives the advertising identifier only with your permission.
  • TMDB (themoviedb.org) — supplies all film and TV data. Requests for titles, posters, and search results go to TMDB, so TMDB sees the requests the app makes. See their privacy policy.
  • OpenWeather — receives approximate coordinates only if you enable the weather feature.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data, and there is no CineFlicx database of users to sell.

Your choices

  • Tracking. iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Location, notifications, photos. iOS Settings → CineFlicx.
  • Your library. Remove individual titles in the app, or delete the app to remove everything at once.
  • Search history. Cleared from within the app's search screen.

Because your library is only ever on your device, a data export or deletion request has nothing on our side to act on — you already hold all of it, and deleting the app destroys it.

Children

CineFlicx is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. The app surfaces film and TV content that includes mature themes, and its age rating reflects that. An adult-content preference is available in the app and is off by default.

Changes

If we materially change what is collected or how it's used, we'll update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
