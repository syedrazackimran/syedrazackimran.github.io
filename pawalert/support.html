PawAlert Support

Need help with the app, want to report a bug, or have a feature request? You're in the right place.

Contact

Email isyedrazackimran@gmail.com. We typically respond within 2–3 business days.

When you write in, please include:

  • The model and iOS version of your device.
  • The PawAlert app version (Profile → About).
  • A clear description of what happened, what you expected, and what you saw instead. Screenshots help a lot.

Common questions

I signed up but I'm not getting any push notifications.

Three things to check, in order:

  1. iOS Settings → Notifications → PawAlert — make sure Allow Notifications is on.
  2. In-app Profile → Notifications — verify your radius and the severities you want to be alerted about. Quiet hours suppress non-emergency alerts.
  3. Sign out and back in. This refreshes your push token. If alerts start working after that, write to support so we can investigate.

How do I report an animal in distress?

Open the app, tap Report, take or pick a photo, choose severity, confirm the location (defaults to your current GPS), optionally add a short voice note, and Submit. Volunteers in the area get notified.

I accepted a rescue but can't get there. What happens?

Tap Release on the rescue — the report goes back into the queue and nearby volunteers are alerted again. If you don't release manually and no activity is recorded for ~45 minutes, the system releases it for you automatically.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete Account. This is permanent and:

  • Releases any rescue you've accepted.
  • Marks your unresolved reports as closed.
  • Deletes your FCM tokens.
  • Removes your user document.

Photos and reports are anonymized — they remain associated with the deleted user ID but no longer linked to you.

Where can I see all my past reports?

Reports tab → Resolved. Both reports you've filed and rescues you've completed are listed.

I see a report but the location looks wrong.

The location is captured from the reporter's GPS at the moment of posting. If it's clearly off, treat the address title and landmark as the source of truth, and feel free to mark the rescue complete with a note explaining the actual location. We're tracking GPS-accuracy improvements for a future update.

Privacy — who can see my reports and my profile?

Any signed-in PawAlert user can see your reports (location, photo, display name) — that's the rescue coordination model. Your phone number, email, and other profile details are private to you. Full detail in our Privacy Policy.

Reporting a bug

Email support with the device/iOS/app version info above plus the sequence of steps that triggered the bug. If the app crashed, the next time you launch it, an automatic crash report is sent — please mention the approximate date/time so we can match it.

Feature requests

We read everything. Email is the right channel. We don't promise to build every request, but a good feature description with the use case that motivated it goes a long way.
