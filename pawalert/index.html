PawAlert

Alert neighbors. Save animals.

PawAlert turns your phone into a rescue lifeline for street animals. See a hurt dog, an injured cat, or an animal in distress? Snap a photo, drop a pin, and nearby volunteers are alerted in real time.

How it works

  1. Spot an animal that needs help.
  2. Report with a photo, severity, and location — takes seconds.
  3. Nearby volunteers get notified within seconds.
  4. A volunteer accepts the rescue and heads out.
  5. They mark it complete with a proof photo when the animal is safe.

You can be a Reporter, a Volunteer, or both — switch any time from the Profile tab.

Why PawAlert

  • Real time, not a hotline queue. Alerts go to phones in the area, not a call center.
  • Built around proximity. Volunteers see only what's near them; you set the radius, severity, and quiet hours.
  • Closed loop. Reporters see who's coming, when they accepted, and the proof when the rescue finishes.
  • Free. No ads. No subscription. No tracking SDKs.
